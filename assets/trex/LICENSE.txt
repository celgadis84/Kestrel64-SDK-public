the LibSaturn project (dino_demo example). SHA-256:
207694403B987B6A465107524754D8E0D100174C85B214825E74BEFF492484D1

kestrel64-sdk redistributes it under that license. At build time
tools/k64bake.py (tools/k64model.py) derives the N64 model blob from it
(demo 10_trex): the mesh is decimated (10902 -> 2400 triangles), its textures
are packed into one 64x64 4-bit indexed atlas, the skin weights are reduced to
two joints in quarter steps and the animation is resampled at 30 fps and
quantized. These changes were made by kestrel64-sdk; pikabobalex did not create
or endorse the conversion or the demo.
